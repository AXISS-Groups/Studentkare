Flow 09 · Points and offers

Money never touches the body.

This is the flow most likely to corrupt the product, so it is built with a wall down the middle. Points are earned by finishing things and never by a measurement improving. The offers service cannot read the clinical database. And the whole surface uses a colour that appears nowhere near a clinical value, so if the two ever share a screen it is visibly wrong.

Dev notes and edge cases

Most of this flow is a set of refusals. They are worth enforcing in code rather than in a policy document.
THE WALL
Offers cannot read the clinical database
Enforced in the service layer, not in policy. The offers service has no read grant on records, so a targeted health offer is not something a future sprint can accidentally ship.
EARNING
Actions, never measurements
Points come from finishing a camp, closing a referral, reading an article. No value in a report has ever earned or cost a point, and no metric is rewarded for improving.
CARE FIRST
Health redemptions sit above the marketplace
Lab tests and consults are the first screen; food and gyms are one tap further. If the ordering ever inverts, the incentive has inverted with it.
DISCLOSURE
Partners get campus and year
Shown to the student on the redemption screen, in the same words every time. A partner never learns the app was used for anything medical.
NO GAME
No streak, tier, ring or leaderboard
A ledger with dates and amounts. Students under exam pressure do not need another thing to keep up, and a streak on a health app punishes illness.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Partner refuses a valid code · offer withdrawn after redemption · student graduates mid-offer · points earned on a camp later voided · code shared with a friend · partner asks for more data as a condition.
