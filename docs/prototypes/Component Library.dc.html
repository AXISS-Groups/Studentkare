Rules that outrank the components

A screen can use every component here correctly and still be wrong. These are the ones to check in review.
No score, ring or gauge
No health score, no BMI dial, no percentile, no streak. Body metrics are recorded and referred, never rated.
Money never touches the body
Points, offers and rewards never appear on a screen containing a clinical value. The reward colour exists so this is visible.
Every clinical view ends in a human
Book, escalate or see a doctor. A screen showing a value with no next step is unfinished.
The source is always one tap away
Every clinical number came off a document. View original is present on every record detail.
Ranges come off the printout
Never a model-supplied or app-supplied reference range. If it could not be read, show the scan and leave the value blank.
Nothing near mental health
No badge, count, streak or reminder on any counselling or crisis surface. No notification category exists.
