Rules

Short, because a mark that needs a long manual is not working.
Never on red
Emergency surfaces get the bare outline at 18px — no fill, no letters. A gradient shield competes with the only content that matters there, and SK is illegible at that size anyway.
Never rotate or recolour the faces
The two-tone fold is the identity. Flat fills are fine — that is the mono variant — but a third colour or a tilt is not.
Strapline dies below 40px
It stops being legible before it stops fitting. Header, favicon and app icon all drop it.
One italic word
Only "Kare" is italic serif. Setting both words in it turns a signature into a wedding invitation.
Clear space is half the shield
On every side. In a tab bar or a station header, that space is what keeps it legible rather than decorative.
SVG only
No PNG in the product. The mark ships as SVG so 16px and 2m are the same file, and mono is a fill change rather than a second asset.
