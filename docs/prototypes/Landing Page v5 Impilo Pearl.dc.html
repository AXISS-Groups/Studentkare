AI and security

The AI reads your report back to you. That is the whole job.

It restates what is printed, cites the page it came from, and says when it cannot tell. Anything that looks like diagnosis, prediction or advice is out of bounds by design, not by disclaimer.

What it does
Reads a photographed report and files it on your timeline
Restates each value in plain language, in your language
Marks a value outside the range printed on that same page
Plots a value you already have, each point labelled with its source
Explains what a test measures, in general terms
Ends every clinical-adjacent answer with a way to reach a doctor
What it never does
Diagnose, screen, predict or estimate a risk
Supply a reference range of its own
Guess a value it could not read — it shows you the scan instead
Advise on dosage, substitution or stopping a medicine
Give a diet, weight or calorie target
Reply to distress with a model-written answer
The crisis gate

Every free-text box in the product passes a classifier before it reaches any model. If it fires, the model is bypassed entirely and fixed, reviewed content appears.

How the data is held
Per-user envelope encryption
Every student's documents sit under their own key, not one shared bucket key.
Region-locked processing
Indian users are processed in Indian regions. A provider that cannot do that is not a candidate.
Zero retention with model providers
No provider-side prompt logging, and no training on your health data. Written into the contract.
Identity stripped before inference
Name, phone, address, ABHA and roll number are removed. The model gets values, not a person.
Append-only audit log
Every consent, access and AI output is logged with its model and prompt version. Nothing is edited after the fact.
Clinician tools are a separate system
The doctor-facing review engine has its own auth realm and no route into a student session.

What we will not do with your records

Never used to train models
Your health data is read to answer you, then dropped. It is in the contract with our providers.
Never used to target offers
The offers service cannot read the clinical database. Points and reports never share a screen.
Never scored or ranked
No health score, no BMI gauge, no comparison to other students. Values are recorded, not rated.
Yours to take or erase
Itemised consent under the DPDP Act, export any time, and deletion that actually deletes.

Questions

The ones worth answering honestly.

How do you actually make money?
Institutions pay per student per year for camp software, clinician review and compliance reporting. Incubators pay per seat for cover. Partner discounts pay a referral fee that is never conditional on anything in your records. That is the whole model.
Can my college see my results?
No. It sees that you attended a checkup, and counts by year and department. No value, no finding, no name against a referral. Any group smaller than twenty is not broken out at all, because eleven students with three referrals identifies people.
Is my data used to train AI?
No. Your health data is read to answer you and then dropped — it is a contractual term with our providers, not a policy line. Identity is stripped before any text reaches a model, and everything is processed in Indian regions.
Does the app diagnose anything?
No, and it is built so it cannot drift into doing so. It reads what is printed on your documents and shows the range the lab printed beside it. Any interpretation comes from a registered practitioner whose registration number is attached to what they said.
What happens when I graduate?
Nothing changes. Your records are yours, not your college's. Add a personal email before your final year so you do not lose access when the college address is deactivated — we remind you.
Why is there no health score?
Because a score invites someone to ask for it. Once a number exists, an insurer or an employer can make it a condition. Values are recorded and referred to a doctor; they are never rated, ranked or compared to other students.
Can I get everything out?
Yes, any time, in one export: original scans, every value read, and a machine-readable file another health app can import. Deletion removes your records within 30 days. Two things survive because the law requires it — the access log and camp attendance already reported.
What if I lose my phone?
Your records are on the server, encrypted under a key that is yours. A new device needs a code on two channels before it can open them. Your emergency card also exists as a printed card, which is the point of printing it.
