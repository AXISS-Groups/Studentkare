Flow 06 · Emergency and crisis

The two screens that cannot afford a single wasted pixel.

Both are read by someone under pressure — a stranger holding a phone they did not unlock, or a student at 2am who has run out of other options. Neither screen animates on entry, neither has app chrome, and neither needs a login. Red appears nowhere else in the product so that when it appears here it means one thing.

Dev notes and edge cases

These two screens have the strictest constraints in the product, and most of them are about what must not happen.
OUTSIDE AUTH
Never behind a session
Served from local storage and a signed public link. Lockout, token expiry, logout and a deleted account must all leave the card readable — it is the one surface that survives everything.
ZERO MOTION
The card does not animate
No entry transition, no fade, no spinner. It paints in one frame at full brightness. The lock-screen ring is the only motion, and it is on the button, not the card.
PROVENANCE
Stated, not diagnosed
Every line is the student's own words with the date they said it. Nothing is pulled from a report, because a responder acting on a stale inference is worse than one acting on none.
CRISIS
Never triggered by inference
The takeover is only ever opened by the student. No sentiment model, no keyword scan, no behavioural trigger — guessing a mental state from app usage is not a feature we are willing to ship.
SILENCE
No telemetry on the crisis path
No analytics event, no notification, no badge, no entry in the records timeline. The notification service has no read grant on counselling events, so the category cannot be added later by mistake.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Card opened by a curious friend · printed card still in a wallet after the allergy list changed · student with no emergency contact · phone at 1% brightness in sunlight · responder who reads only Telugu · helpline busy at 2am · student deletes the account while a printed card is circulating.
