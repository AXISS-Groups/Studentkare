Under the DPDP Act you can
Ask what we hold · correct it · withdraw any consent · take everything out · have it erased · nominate someone to act for you · complain to the grievance officer, and then to the Board.
Dev notes and edge cases
Settings is where most of the legal surface area lives. These are the parts that are obligations rather than preferences.
WITHDRAWAL
As easy as consent, literally
Same number of taps to turn off as to turn on. No confirmation modal that consent never had, no retention offer, no "you will lose" copy.
AGE
Read-only, forever
There is no editable date-of-birth field anywhere in the product. Age comes from the verification token and changing it means re-verifying.
FAMILY
A profile is a grant, not a copy
The parent owns their records. Managing is a revocable grant with its own consent artefact, and on account deletion the grant unwinds before anything is erased.
ERASURE
Name what survives
The audit log and reported camp attendance are retained for legal reasons. Say so on the screen rather than in a policy nobody opens.
NOTIFICATIONS
No mental-health category exists
Enforce at the service layer: the notification service has no read grant on counselling events, so the category cannot be added by mistake later.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
College email deactivated after graduation · number reassigned to someone else by the telco · parent wants their records back after a falling-out · student deletes the account then reinstalls · export requested while an upload is still queued · language switched mid-consult.
