Dev notes and edge cases

The constraints here are the product. A console that leaks one student's result destroys the trust the whole platform runs on.
AGGREGATE ONLY
Counts, never values
The console has no read grant on clinical values at all — not filtered, not redacted, absent. An administrator cannot see a result even by mistake, because the query does not exist.
SMALL GROUPS
No cell under twenty
A department of eleven with three referrals identifies people. Any breakdown below the threshold collapses into a larger group rather than being shown.
ATTENDANCE
The one per-student field
A college has a legitimate need to know who attended a mandatory checkup. That is the whole per-student surface: attended, or did not.
REPORTING
The return comes from the same data
AICTE and UGC returns generate from the counts already on screen. No spreadsheet, no re-keying, and no second version of the truth to reconcile.
PAIRING
Stations sync the night before
The roster is pushed to paired tablets by 20:00 so camp day never depends on the venue wifi. An unpaired station is shown as a blocking warning, not a footnote.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Camp cancelled after publishing · a student transfers department mid-year · tablet paired then lost · department too small to report · college asks for the referral list · roster contains students who have left.
