Dev notes and edge cases

The commercial pressure here is different from the campus: the payer is closer to the person, and more interested in them.
SAME RULES
A cohort is a campus
Founders get the identical guarantee students do: records are theirs, the incubator sees counts, and cover leaves with them. Reusing the model rather than inventing a B2B variant is what keeps the promise checkable.
NO SCORE
The founder check gives no number
Six self-assessed statements, written back as a restatement. No wellbeing score, no band, no traffic light — a score invites the incubator to ask for it, and once asked it becomes a condition of funding.
NEVER UPSTREAM
Answers do not reach the console
Not aggregated, not anonymised, not at all. A 68-company cohort is small enough that almost any breakdown identifies someone, so counselling reports as a single number.
THE PAYER PROBLEM
The company pays, and still sees nothing
A founder pays for a teammate's cover and can see it was used, never what for. The teammate is told exactly this at the point they join, in the same words.
CONTINUITY
Cover survives the startup
90 days after a shutdown, because the moment a founder most needs a counsellor is the month the company dies. This is a commercial term with a design consequence: no screen ever says "your access has ended".
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Founder in two cohorts at once · startup exits the incubator mid-year · cofounder dispute over billing · teammate leaves and takes their records · incubator asks for a per-startup wellbeing breakdown · founder is also a student on a campus plan.
