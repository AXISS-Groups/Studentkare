Dev notes and edge cases

The decisions in this flow that are judgement calls rather than layout.
SCOPED ACCESS
Per-consult, per-record, self-expiring
A share grant names the doctor, the records and a 24-hour window after the consult. No standing access, no "share my whole vault" option — the widest grant a student can make is still one consult.
ALWAYS SHARED
Allergies override the toggle
Allergies, conditions and current medicines go with every consult regardless of what is ticked. A doctor prescribing blind is the one risk the flow does not let a student take.
VERIFICATION
Registration number, linked
Every practitioner card carries a council registration that resolves to the public register. If a number cannot be verified the doctor is not listed — there is no unverified tier.
REFERRALS
Open until closed by a human
A referral persists on the Care tab with its age in days. It closes when a consult happens or the student says they saw someone else. It is never auto-closed by time, and the college is never told.
PRESCRIPTIONS
Structured, not a photo
Drug, dose, frequency and duration are separate fields so they can be searched, checked against allergies and reordered. A signed PDF is generated from them, not the reverse.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Doctor joins late · student on 2G in a hostel · consult ends before records load · prescription for a drug the student is allergic to · student books for a family profile · slot booked during a class · phlebotomist cannot find the hostel gate.
