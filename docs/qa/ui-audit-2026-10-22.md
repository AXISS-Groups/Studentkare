## Found but not changed

- **Rate limit vs campus NAT.** The global limiter is 100 requests/minute **per IP**
  (`backend/core/rate_limiter.py`, `RATE_LIMIT_API_PER_MINUTE`). One student page fires 10–20 calls, so
  browsing a few pages in a minute returned 429s and "Your session could not be checked" during this audit.
  On a campus where hundreds of students share one NAT address, that limit will be hit almost immediately.
  Needs a decision (per-account limiting, or a much higher per-IP ceiling); the audit raised it for the
  local run only.
- **Unmounted features calling endpoints that don't exist:** `MedicalScannerViewModel`
  (`/ai/medication-search`), `IncidentTriageViewModel` (`/incidents/*`), `LifeShareViewModel`
  (`/lifeshare/*`), `TeleconsultViewModel` (`/teleconsult/eprescription`). No route renders them, so users never
  see them; they are listed in the contract test's `UNMOUNTED` set to delete or wire up.
- Labs, Trends, Medicines today, Water and Steps at 1440 are the phone boards in a narrow page column.
  Deliberate today (no desktop boards yet) but it reads as "mobile on desktop".
- The demo seed has no knowledge sources, orders, bookings, lab orders, students-on-campus or hospitals, so
  most consoles open on their empty states. A richer demo seed would make reviews like this one less
  misleading.
