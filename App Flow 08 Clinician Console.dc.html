Dev notes and edge cases

This console carries clinical and regulatory weight. Most of these are constraints, not features.
NOT A DIAGNOSIS
Trends, sources, no inferred cause
The console surfaces a pattern across documents and cites each one. It never names a condition, suggests a cause or ranks likelihood. Ordering is by distance from the printed range, not by model confidence.
SIGN-OFF
Every release carries a registration number
Nothing reaches a student unsigned. The practitioner's number is attached to the decision and written into the student's own access log, so they can see who acted and when.
PLAIN LANGUAGE
The student reads the note, not the flag
The clinical flag stays internal. What the student sees is a sentence a person wrote, which is why the note field is required on any decision other than release-with-no-action.
PROVENANCE
Every value links to its scan
Four readings from three sources, each one openable. A clinician acting on a number they cannot trace is the failure this panel exists to prevent.
SCOPE
Camp cohort only, and time-boxed
Access is granted for one camp and expires when the review closes. A clinician cannot browse the student body, and the college never sees any of this.
EDGE CASES
The ones that will happen
Volunteer mistyped a reading · student already saw a doctor privately · two students with the same surname · reviewer disagrees with a prior sign-off · student withdraws consent mid-review · camp value contradicts a lab report from the same week.
