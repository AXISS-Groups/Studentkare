For help with the app, open Help (Support in the menu). The Help centre lists common questions: where your order is, why a medicine needs a prescription, who can see your health records, how campus verification works, and deleting your account.

Still stuck? Choose Message support, write a subject and your message, and send it. A person reads every message. Your messages are listed under Your messages, with whether each one is open or resolved. No reply time is promised.

Support helps with orders, deliveries and your account. Anything clinical is answered by a registered clinician, not by support: book a consult for that.

Crisis support never waits for support. Call 112 in an emergency, or Tele-MANAS on 14416, free and confidential, day and night.

Privacy, terms and the grievance officer are linked from the Help centre too.
