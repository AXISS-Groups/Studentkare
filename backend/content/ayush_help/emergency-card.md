Your emergency card holds what a stranger or a doctor needs in the first minutes: your blood group, allergies, long-term conditions, and an emergency contact. You decide what goes on it. Open it from Emergency card in the menu, or from your care profile.

Blood group is Not set unless you choose it. Pick it only if a report or a doctor confirmed it, because a wrong blood group is worse than none. For allergies, list medicines, foods or anything else you react to, and leave it empty if you do not know. For the emergency contact, add a name, a 10-digit Indian mobile number and the relationship. One contact is supported for now.

Choose Save card to keep your changes. The card shows how ready it is: a blood group that is set, and at least one reachable contact. You can print the card or save it as a PDF; save your changes first so the printed card matches.

Who can see this card: you, when you are signed in. No one else can open it from a link or a QR code, because there is no QR code or offline copy of the card yet. It is never shown to shops or partners.

Your emergency contact is also one of the people an SOS reaches. See the article on SOS.
