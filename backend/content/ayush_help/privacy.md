Your health records belong to you. Reports, prescriptions and visit notes stay in one account you own, and stay yours after you graduate.

Who can see your records: only you, unless you share a document. Sharing is per document and time-limited, and you can stop any share at any time. See the article on consent and sharing.

Your college's campus health office sees your enrolment status only, never your records or other health data. Your campus is not told what you order, and it never sees what you discussed with a doctor.

Shops, pharmacies and labs see only what they need to fulfil an order, such as a delivery address. They never see anything from your health records, and paying for something gives no one access to your records. There is no advertising based on your health, and your health data is never sold.

Your location, if you choose to share it in Find care, stays on your device. It is not sent to StudentKare or stored.

Ayush cannot read your health records. What you ask Ayush is kept only if you turned on Ayush history in your choices, only you can read it, and deleting your account erases it.

Your emergency card is visible only to you when you are signed in.

Your files are checked for viruses before they are saved. To see or download everything we hold, use Download my data. To delete your account, see the article on deleting your account. For any privacy question, send a message from Help.
