Open Invite friends to see your invite code and link. Share the link from your phone's share options, or choose Copy link and paste it into a chat with your friend.

You get {{setting:rewards.referral_points}} points when a friend joins with your code and is verified as a student. Up to {{setting:rewards.referral_monthly_cap}} friends a month earn you points.

The screen shows three numbers: how many people signed up with your code, how many were verified as students, and how many earned you points. You see numbers only, never who joined.

Points have no cash value. They cannot be exchanged, transferred or spent on anything yet, and they cannot be used to pay for an order.

Medals mark things you have done in StudentKare, such as verifying your profile, completing your emergency card, attending your first health camp, or inviting a friend who joined. Only you can see your medals. They never compare you with anyone.

Invite friends is for student accounts.
