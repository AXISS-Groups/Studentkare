In an emergency, call 112. It is India's emergency number for police, fire and ambulance. It is free, works day and night, and you never need a StudentKare account to call it.

For an ambulance, call 108. It is the emergency medical transport service, free, day and night.

For mental health support, call Tele-MANAS on 14416, or the toll-free line 1-800-891-4416. Tele-MANAS is a free and confidential Government of India service, open day and night, in English, Hindi, Telugu and other languages. You can call if you feel overwhelmed, anxious, low, or not safe.

If someone has swallowed, breathed in or touched something harmful, the National Poison Helpline is 1800-116-117.

These numbers are one tap away on every StudentKare screen, even when you are signed out. Your campus counsellor is also free and private.

If you might act on thoughts of harming yourself, or someone is in danger right now, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 now, and use the SOS button to alert your campus.
