Ayush is StudentKare's assistant for questions about how StudentKare works. Ayush answers only from help articles and sources that the StudentKare team has approved, and shows which article each answer came from. Ayush is not a doctor.

Ayush will not diagnose or name a condition, will not prescribe a medicine or suggest a dose, and cannot read anything in your health vault, such as your reports or results. For those, book a consult with a doctor, or ask for a doctor to review a report.

If Ayush cannot find an answer in the approved help, it says so instead of guessing. You can then open Help to message support, or book a consult.

If you say something that sounds like a crisis, Ayush shows emergency and Tele-MANAS helpline numbers straight away, and your campus follow-up team may be told so someone can check on you.

Your chats with Ayush are saved only if you turn on Ayush history in your choices. Only you can read it, and deleting your account erases it.
