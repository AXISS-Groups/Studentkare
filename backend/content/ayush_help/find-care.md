Find care lists hospitals, pharmacies and ambulance services that someone has checked, and doctors you can book. Open it from Find care in the menu.

At the top, the emergency numbers are always shown: call 112 for police, fire and medical emergencies, and 108 for an ambulance.

Use the tabs to choose Hospitals, Pharmacies, Ambulance or Doctors. Each place shows its type, such as hospital, clinic, pharmacy, ambulance service or blood bank, whether it is open 24 hours, whether it is your campus or a StudentKare partner, and when it was last checked. Choose Call to phone it or Directions to open your maps app.

To see the nearest places first, choose Use my location. Your location stays on your device; it is not sent to StudentKare or stored. Without it, the list is in name order. You can stop using your location at any time.

On the Doctors tab you see each doctor's price and open slots, and you can book. Sign in to see open slots and book.

If a list is empty, StudentKare and your campus have not added a place there yet. In an emergency, call 112 or 108.
