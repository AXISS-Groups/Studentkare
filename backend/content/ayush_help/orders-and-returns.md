Your orders are under My orders, split into Active and Past. Each order shows the step it is at: Order placed, Payment, Accepted by the provider, Dispatched, and Completed. This shows which step your order has reached, not a live location.

You can cancel an order yourself while that option is shown. If the cancel does not go through, the order may have just been paid or accepted, so contact support. If the provider declines your order and you had paid, support arranges the refund. A cancelled order needs nothing more from you.

To return an item, choose Return on the order, or open My requests and choose Return an item. Items can be returned once they have been dispatched or delivered. Say what is wrong, and add a photo if you can, which helps the partner decide. Photos must be PNG or JPEG, up to 5 MB.

If a return is approved, the refund is arranged with the partner and goes back the way you paid. StudentKare does not hold or move the money.

My requests also lists your hostel room visits and medicine refill requests, and shows where each one is up to. For help with any order, choose Get help on it or open Help.
