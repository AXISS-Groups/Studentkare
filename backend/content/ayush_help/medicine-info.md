Medicine info lets you look up a medicine by its name or by what is in it. Open Medicine info from search on Home, from Medicines in the shop, or from About this medicine on a product. You can also open it without signing in.

Search for the brand name or the salt printed on the strip. Search allows for small spelling mistakes. Pick the one that matches your strip. A chip shows whether it needs a prescription, and whether a pharmacist has reviewed it.

You can also photograph the strip or box, or type what is printed on it. Your photo is read once and not stored. You then pick which medicine is on your strip: nothing is chosen for you, and Medicine info never guesses from how a tablet looks. If reading photos is not switched on yet, type what is printed instead.

A medicine page shows what it contains, its form, pack and maker. What it is used for, common side effects, key warnings and how to store it are shown only after a pharmacist has reviewed them, each with its source and review date. Nothing in Medicine info is written by AI.

Who is it for? lets you pick an age group to see which forms and strengths the reviewed label lists. Your choice stays on your device. It is what the label lists, not a dose: your doctor or pharmacist tells you how much and how often. For a baby, a child, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding, ask a doctor or pharmacist first.

If we have no reviewed information for a medicine yet, Medicine info says so. Ask a pharmacist, or book a consult. Take medicines only as prescribed by a doctor or as advised by a pharmacist.
