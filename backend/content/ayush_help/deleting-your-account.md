You can delete your StudentKare account from Delete my account, in your account settings. Before you go, you can download your data first: your documents, readings and policies as one file.

To confirm, type the word the screen asks for. Your account is then scheduled for deletion in {{setting:privacy.deletion_grace_days}} days. Until that date everything works as normal, and you can cancel by choosing Cancel, keep my account. Crisis support and your emergency card keep working until then.

On that date your account and everything in it are removed from StudentKare: records, reports, prescriptions, readings, orders and messages. After that you cannot sign in, and nothing can be recovered by you.

For legal and safety reasons a sealed, encrypted copy is kept for a limited period. Only a super administrator can open it, only with a recorded reason, and then it is destroyed. The audit log keeps a record that actions happened, with a random ID and never your name.

Leaving college? You can switch to a personal account instead of deleting. If you are worried about who sees what, review your sign-in and security settings and your shares first. If you are going through a hard time, you can still delete your account, and you can also talk to Tele-MANAS on 14416, free and confidential, any hour.
