The red SOS button is on every main screen. If you are in danger right now, call 112 first. Calling 112 needs no account and works even when you are signed out.

When you are signed in, Send SOS alerts your campus. Who gets it: your campus console gets an alert, campus security gets a WhatsApp message, and your emergency contact gets a message. You can add where you are, for example Library, 2nd floor, but that is optional. A short countdown lets you cancel before it is sent.

After you send it, the screen shows exactly who was alerted and whether each message was sent, not delivered, or not set up. It also shows when your campus has seen your SOS. If nobody could be reached, your SOS is still recorded, but call 112 straight away. If sending takes long, do not wait; call 112.

When you are safe, choose I'm safe now to close your SOS. Your campus can also close it, and you can see their note.

If you have no emergency contact yet, add one on your emergency card so an SOS can reach them. Tele-MANAS on 14416 is free and open day and night for anyone shaken after an emergency.
