HOW WE KEEP IT SAFETraining that is good for you, not just hard
Readiness check firstA short health questionnaire before your first session. Anything flagged goes to a campus clinician, not the coach.
No weight, no caloriesWe don’t track body weight, calories or body shape here. Progress means turning up and feeling better.
No leaderboardsAttendance is private to you. Nobody sees who came, who didn’t, or how much you lifted.
Hurt? Straight to physioPain during a session ends the session. The sports medicine room sees you the same day.
Private by default Records open only to you and the clinician you choose. Your campus sees counts, never results.
Verified clinicians Every doctor is NMC-registered and every lab NABL-accredited before they can list.
Near your hostel Collection at your block, consults between classes, a campus clinic when it is open.
Price before you book Every price is on screen before you confirm. A plan changes the price, never the care.
