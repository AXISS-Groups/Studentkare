Who we areStudent Kare is run by AVKS AI (Araxis Systems), Hyderabad. We are the “data fiduciary” for your account under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
What we collectYour name, contact details, college and roll number; your photo and ID checks; the health records you add or that doctors and labs send you; orders and payments. Aadhaar: only the last 4 digits and your name from DigiLocker.
WhyTo give you care you ask for, keep your records together, verify it’s you at pharmacies, labs and clinics, and keep you safe (SOS, emergency card). Never to target ads — partners can’t see your health.
Who sees itYou decide who sees your records, share by share, for a set time. Your college sees enrolment and headcounts only. Staff who scan your pass see your photo and what that visit needs.
How long we keep itWhile your account is open. After you delete it, we erase your data within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep (payment records 8 years).
Your rightsSee, correct, download or erase your data; withdraw any consent; name someone to act for you. Use the Grievance tab — we reply within 30 days.
