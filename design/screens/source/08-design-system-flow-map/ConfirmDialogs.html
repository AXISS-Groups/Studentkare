DESIGN SYSTEM · CONFIRM DIALOGSAsk once, clearly, before anything you can’t undo.
ANATOMY
Icon — soft round badge, pops in. Amber = caution, red = destructive, indigo = privacy. Title — a direct question that names the thing: “Cancel this lab booking?” Body — one or two lines: what happens, what doesn’t, and money if any. Buttons — the safe choice on the left, outlined. The action on the right, filled, with a focus ring. Labels — say what they do: “Keep booking” / “Cancel booking”, never “No / Yes”. After — the button spins while it works, then the dialog becomes a small success card.
Web: centred dialog over a dimmed page. Mobile: the same content as a bottom sheet. Esc and the backdrop close it — except while the action is running.
