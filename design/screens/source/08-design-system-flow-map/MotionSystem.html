2 · LOADERS — PICK BY HOW LONG AND HOW CERTAIN
Spinner Under 2s, inside a button or a small area. Never full-screen — a spinner with nothing around it tells you nothing.
Indeterminate bar A route is changing and we cannot say how long. Sits at the top of the surface, never blocks what is already readable.
Skeleton Content whose shape we already know — a list, a card grid. Match the real layout or it reads as a glitch when it swaps.
Thinking dots Ayush composing an answer. Only where a human would reasonably expect a pause — never to disguise a slow query.

4 · FOCUS & PRESS — NOW ON EVERY CONTROL
Focused Pressed Focused, secondary 3px ring, 3px offset, on :focus-visible only Keyboard users get the ring; mouse users never see it. Press drops the control 1px, so a tap is acknowledged before the route changes — which matters most on the slow campus wifi this is used on.
5 · REDUCED MOTION

Every animation has an off switch we do not control.

Vestibular disorders, migraine and motion sensitivity are common in this age group. The system setting is honoured everywhere — all 98 artboards — with no in-app toggle to find.
